## Implemented Task 4 identity and booking foundation

The owner subsequently authorized coding the models whose decisions are clear. This first implementation is **partial**, not completion of the coverage matrix. See the [implementation and recovery record](reviews/TASK4_MODEL_FOUNDATION_2026-10-03.md) and [Task 4 evidence](TASKS.md#4-cross-app-model-completion).

- `CustomerAccount` supplies an opaque UUID, display name, lifecycle and closure timestamp outside Django staff auth. Closure cannot reassign/reopen that identity or remove booking history. There are no customer credentials, phone lookup, authentication properties or login endpoints yet; a model row is not an authenticated principal.
- `TrialBooking` contains immutable checkout mode/ownership, market, recipient name/phone/address/country/PIN, request UUID and fingerprint. PostgreSQL rejects changing GUEST/null to ACCOUNT/non-null even in one SQL update; account ownership is equally immutable. Phone is neither unique nor an account key. Only DRAFT intent is currently representable: this is not the final accepted-booking lifecycle.
- The original contact snapshot is immutable even during this initial draft-only implementation. Corrected input currently means new intent, not mutation of an OTP-bound phone. Versioned amendments, accepted states and their policy/verification references still belong to Task 4 completion; no production amendment policy is chosen here.
- `TrialBox` supplies the protected booking/category container, without a unique booking/category constraint. Plan/item/quantity/physical-allocation persistence remains incomplete; the ability to represent repeated categories is not approval to offer them.
- `BookingPhoneChallenge` and `ArrivalOTP` are separate purpose-bound tables, with encoded secret hashes, explicit expiry/max attempts, monotonic attempts and verification/consumption/invalidation evidence. Arrival references an assignment; both bind the phone to the original booking. Row locking and SQL guards preserve outcomes. No generation, sending, secret comparison, challenge replacement or business transition is implemented; model timestamps alone do not prove verification or authorize payment.
- `GuestBookingAccessGrant` retains a digest, one guest booking, issuance/expiry and one-way revocation. No account FK or phone-history lookup exists. Issuance, authorization, recovery and security configuration remain unfinished.
- `PolicyDocumentRevision` retains version/language/content and a computed SHA-256; `BookingPolicyAcceptance` references that immutable version with acceptance time and request evidence, never CustomerAccount. Content is not approved legal wording, and booking acceptance is not marketing consent.
- `DeliveryAssignment` retains agent, assigning staff, assignment/end timestamps and reason. One open assignment per booking is database-enforced; a replacement is a new row, not an overwritten agent. Field agents need not have Django Admin's `is_staff` flag. This storage does not authorize dispatch, enforce operational RBAC, start a visit or settle an order.
- New identity/evidence tables have protected FKs and ORM/database history guards. Existing staff/audit/catalogue/inventory history is not relabelled or backfilled. Downgrades that would remove new evidence guards are refused when their tables contain records; production retention/erasure still needs LR-04 and controlled implementation, not blanket deletion or indefinite-retention claims.

No new endpoint, Admin editing surface, published URL, metadata or frontend change accompanies these private models. They must remain excluded from future public catalogue projections and search surfaces. This implementation supplies no launch policy, financial ledger or compliance certification.

## Implemented Task 2 boundary

- Every reference has a public UUID; market, hub, material, category and product codes are stable unique identifiers separate from display names. Purity codes are unique within material. A variant has a unique opaque SKU; business logic never parses it. Size/specification belong to the variant, while every physical piece has a different InventoryUnit UUID.
- Reference statuses are DRAFT, ACTIVE, COMING_SOON and INACTIVE. New rows default to DRAFT. Separate seed migrations insert only approved BLR/Bengaluru/ACTIVE, HYD/Hyderabad/COMING_SOON, SILVER/ACTIVE, GOLD/COMING_SOON and Silver S925. Reversing seed migrations preserves rows; reapplying does not reset edited labels/statuses. No category, product, hub, inventory, PIN, price, tax or policy values are seeded.
- Hub belongs to one immutable market; ServiceArea is scoped by market/country/postal identifier with effective timestamps and approval reference. Several coverage revisions may coexist; exact active/effective matches with evidence yield CONFIGURED, none UNCONFIGURED and overlaps CONFLICT. No routing priority is inferred. Coverage alone never establishes bookability.
- Category trial eligibility reuses BusinessConfiguration. Its exact scope is `{market: <market public UUID>, material: <material public UUID>, category: <category public UUID>}`. All three references must be ACTIVE; no global fallback applies. The returned revision may explicitly say false. A true eligibility value alone is not a trial plan, stock reservation or booking permission.
- Ordinary ORM writes run validation and reject bulk bypasses. Purity must match the variant's material. Used foreign keys use PROTECT. Unit variant/hub and stable codes cannot be reassigned through generic saves. Once units or price revisions reference a variant, its defining specification cannot change, and its product cannot change category. Unit/price creation locks the same variant/product rows as definition edits. These cross-row identity guards are application-level; arbitrary SQL is not a supported onboarding interface.
- At the Task 2 boundary InventoryUnit had only DRAFT status. Task 3 extends it as described below; DRAFT records remain unavailable and no ownership or reservation is inferred.
- PriceRevision is append-only DRAFT evidence, unique per variant/revision. Mode is FIXED or WEIGHT_BASED. Explicit currency, optional fixed amount, weight/unit, rate/unit, rate source, formula reference and typed charge components permit incomplete proposals without inventing values. WEIGHT_BASED cannot have a fixed amount. NUMERIC(24,6) is technical capacity, not an approved currency exponent: reject overflow/extra precision/nonfinite/binary floats; do not round. New revisions preserve previous inputs. ORM guards and a reversible PostgreSQL UPDATE/DELETE trigger protect price history.
- HistoricalPriceSnapshot schema version 1 copies catalogue identity/descriptions, price revision UUID/number, mode/currency, supplied agreed amount, input components and explicit tax evidence. Its defensive JSON serialization is independent of subsequent catalogue/config edits. Tax is either UNCONFIGURED (no invented zero rate) or RECORDED with policy reference and exact components; it does not validate a CA formula or prove payment. **Task 4 must implement transaction snapshot persistence/protection; Tasks 6–8 populate it through workflows** at the approved commitment point. The value object alone is not a database financial ledger.

## Implemented Task 3 additions

- InventoryUnit retains its UUID, variant and hub identity. Status now represents DRAFT, QC_PENDING, AVAILABLE or QUARANTINED, with RETIRED representable but no retirement action enabled. Custody is UNKNOWN or HUB. Draft hub association is intended location, not verified receipt or ownership. Receipt and QC are explicit services; ordinary saves cannot edit state/custody. AVAILABLE requires HUB custody at the database boundary.
- InventoryMovement records REGISTER, RECEIVE, QC_PASS or QC_FAIL, before/after status/hub/custody, actor identity, observed/recorded timestamps, reason/source reference, unique request UUID and fingerprint. Non-registration actions retain a protected procedure revision plus copied approval/scope/value evidence. ORM guards and PostgreSQL triggers reject updates/deletes. Original Task 2 drafts receive no fabricated movements during upgrade. Corrections and later lifecycle actions require future reviewed services.
- ProductMedia belongs to a Product and supports MAIN, ADDITIONAL, VIDEO and THUMBNAIL roles. Thumbnails reference a same-product image/video source. One non-retired main image per product is enforced. Product/role/source/object identity, content type, byte size and SHA-256 are preserved; description, sort order and one-way retirement changes are audited. DRAFT/RETIRED states imply no public publication. Object keys identify private stored assets, not public URLs or business identity.
- CSVImportBatch retains actor, kind, unique request UUID, canonical payload hash, reason, row count and per-row outcome. It is append-only through ORM and PostgreSQL UPDATE/DELETE protection. Unit row registration UUIDs prevent creating the same physical identity on retries; aliases of one UUID are rejected as duplicate rows. These identifiers do not select a barcode/RFID process.
- The configuration registry now also accepts nonblank string `inventory.receiving-procedure-reference` and `inventory.qc-procedure-reference`, using exact scope `{hub: <hub public UUID>}`. No fallback or approved value is provided. Receiving/QC require effective approved evidence, not mere existence of a configuration row. Configuration activation remains a separate reviewed workflow.

## Retained publication additions from original Task 4

- `StorefrontPage` has a public UUID, immutable lowercase hyphenated slug and kind MATERIAL, CATEGORY or PRODUCT. Exactly one corresponding protected OneToOne reference is required by a database check. This gives each subject one canonical page without changing existing catalogue identity. A globally unique slug maps to `/collections/<slug>` for collections and `/products/<slug>` for products; city/material query selections do not create additional product identities.
- Required reviewed title/description and optional SEO title/description are separate from internal catalogue fields. Sort order and featured placement are merchandising data. Status is DRAFT, PUBLISHED or WITHDRAWN, with no published seed content. Published records require withdrawal before edits; identity and subject cannot be reassigned. Ordinary model/queryset writes cannot change publication status or delete a page; these application guards do not claim to prevent privileged direct SQL.
- An optional protected parent supplies navigational hierarchy. Only collection pages can be parents. Validation rejects cycles; PostgreSQL transaction-level advisory locking serializes hierarchy edits before page row locks so concurrent edits cannot create a cycle through supported workflows. Breadcrumb generation walks the relevant ancestor chain rather than loading all published pages. A hidden ancestor is omitted rather than exposing draft content.
- Publish/withdraw actions require active staff, the respective custom permission and a nonblank reason. The service locks the page, checks active subject references for publication and records the state transition and `AuditEvent` in one transaction. Page publication is distinct from Product/Category/Material activation, media publication, approved pricing and business eligibility.
- Public read projections include only published pages whose linked references are active, and active variants/materials/purities. Product title/description come from the reviewed page. SKU, size, material and purity have explicit public fields; arbitrary internal variant specification JSON is withheld. When a product detail request omits material, its active variants supply the presentation context instead of the global catalogue's material default. This does not alter product identity or approve material/box mixing. Exact physical unit/hub identities, counts, ownership, procedure evidence, private media and draft pricing remain private.
- Stock summaries are calculated from selected-market AVAILABLE/HUB units at ACTIVE hubs and contain no commitment record. Every state returns `bookable: false`. `price: {state: UNCONFIGURED}` and an empty media list are intentional current projection boundaries, not a zero price or evidence that private ProductMedia has become public.

Stable slugs are not an implemented redirect-history model. Task 4 must complete required alias/redirect persistence; Task 10's reviewed URL-change workflow must preserve old canonical routes before any rename is enabled. | `TrialBox` | One category-specific container within a booking, with applicable plan/policy snapshot and stable box identity. | Exactly one parent booking and category. **Do not add unique `(booking, category)`**: repeated category boxes remain BD-03. Category does not automatically imply exactly one material. |
| `TrialBoxItem` | Requested variant/selection and quantity/counting facts within one box, linked to later exact-unit allocations. | Every item must satisfy its box category and approved plan. Counting designs/sizes/pieces and duplicate quantities remain BD-03; do not fix one-unit-per-row versus quantity semantics prematurely. |
| `DeliveryAgent` profile / staff scope | Optional operational detail linked to StaffUser, not CustomerAccount; service scope and availability where required. | Reuse staff authentication, not customer authentication. Assignment and market/hub scope require authorization, not merely a role label. Deactivate without deleting historical assignments. |
| `DeliveryAssignment` | Agent assignment and visit/dispatch progress for a booking, with assignment, reassignment and outcome evidence. | Do not fix lifetime unique `booking_id`: reassignment/history and possible later approved revisit need representation. Normally one active combined visit; concurrent or repeat visit behavior remains BD-07. Index `(agent, status, scheduled_at)` where scheduling is approved. |
| Visit / outcome evidence | Combined visit identity, active assignment reference, start/end observations, actor and selection/no-purchase outcome with correction/supersession evidence. | Visit closure does not mean paid, refunded, all units sold or stock returned. Preserve assignment/outcome history; repeated/split visits are not enabled without BD-07. |
| `ArrivalOTP` | Hashed arrival-verification challenge related to the active visit/assignment, generation/expiry, attempts and one-time verification evidence. | No plaintext retained in routine records/logs. Atomic consume/attempt control. One challenge can be delivered on two channels; no separate purchase OTP. Resend/replacement handling remains BD-15 and must not invent revisit policy (BD-07). |
| `DispatchManifest` / `DispatchDocument` | Proposed exact-unit manifest spanning the booking's boxes, linked to dispatch custody and relevant reviewed document snapshot. | Manifest composition must match allocations and selected city. Do not require one originating hub forever or authorize multi-hub sourcing; BD-08 selects the rule. Production format, identity and timing: CA-02/LR-03. |

Cross-row rules such as category agreement, allocation eligibility, market/hub consistency and permitted active assignments require explicit transactional validation and appropriate database enforcement during implementation. A foreign key alone does not enforce those rules.

Historic financial records include confirmed Purchase/PurchaseItem snapshots, verified GatewayTransactions, monetary allocations, deposit applications, issued TaxInvoice/TaxInvoiceItems, executed refunds, settlement evidence and later financial adjustments. Working proposals and attempts may progress, but their transition/evidence history remains attributable. An invoice can show INR 5,000 jewellery total, INR 49 deposit applied and INR 4,951 remaining without redefining jewellery price as INR 4,951; these are illustrative amounts, not approved policy.

Task 4 must enforce precise amounts and currency compatibility, unique provider receipt identity independent of whether business use is booking collection or sale, and explicit links for partial/aggregated settlements. A pending attempt may progress with append-only transition evidence; do not store its mutable status in an immutable receipt row that cannot record later success. Issuance seals both invoice header and line set: no adding/removing/reassigning lines after issue. Protect copies of issuer, recipient and tax facts as well as foreign keys. Later locked workflows prevent over-allocation/refund and duplicate financial confirmation; schema constraints alone cannot implement those workflows.

Upfront collection is required by D-25, but `BookingDeposit` is a logical collection/policy context, not a legal conclusion that every collection is a deposit. Do not choose a fee/deposit classification, tax rate, amount or refund policy without the relevant BD/CA/LR outcome.

## Relationships and constraints that must remain explicit

- Market has hubs and service areas; a physical unit has an operational hub and a product variant. Availability is scoped to the selected market's approved fulfillment operation.
- Product has variants and media. Material and Purity are distinct. Category is the single-category boundary of a TrialBox.
- TrialBooking has TrialBoxes; each has TrialBoxItems; allocation maps requested contents to exact pieces. One normal booking supplies one customer home visit with all its category boxes fulfilled together.
- Guest and account bookings share this graph but never change ownership mode. No matching, claim, consent or analytics path may attach a guest booking to a CustomerAccount. Account closure must preserve a protected non-authenticating identity/tombstone and required transaction evidence under reviewed retention rules, not cascade-delete or silently relabel history.
- Assignment history relates staff to that combined visit. No lifetime uniqueness that erases reassignment, and no automatic support for concurrent split visits.
- Purchases, deposit contexts, payment attempts, allocations, invoices, refunds and settlements remain separate. Their links explain the money and goods history without forcing a commercial cardinality that has not been approved.
- Use protected/restricted deletion for referenced historical records. Retire configuration and staff prospectively. Any personal-data retention/anonymization design must preserve required financial evidence and receive LEGAL REVIEW LR-04, coordinated with CA-02/CA-03, before production; it must not delete history accidentally through cascading relations. Operational retention settings remain BUSINESS DECISION BD-15.
- Timestamps should distinguish occurrence, recording, expiry, confirmation and issuance where relevant. Use timezone-aware storage and explicit display time zones; do not encode business deadlines from the server's local clock.
- Candidate indexes also include reservation expiry/status, active unit commitments, booking reference, payment context/status, gateway references, unprocessed events, outstanding refunds, invoice issuer/series/date and reconciliation status. Final indexes follow actual queries; do not add every field as an index.
- Decide integrity enforcement consciously: ordinary unique/check constraints for row-local facts, conditional uniqueness for applicable active records, locked services for cross-row totals, and migrations/tests to preserve those guarantees. These are proposed mechanisms, not generated DDL.

Task 4 must test these guarantees on PostgreSQL, including ORM/queryset and direct database mutation where database protection is claimed. An `atomic()` wrapper on a stale object or a model `save()` override alone is not a concurrency/history guarantee. No approved model or test may make SOLD stock reservable merely because an old reservation is no longer active. Workflow reauthorization and state rechecks belong to Tasks 6–8.

Before implementing a policy-dependent constraint, check [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). In particular, do not add unique booking/category boxes, lifetime single-hub routing, automatic deposit defaults, a fixed price-lock point, global one-sale-ever-per-unit, or exact booking/purchase/deposit cardinalities merely for implementation convenience.
