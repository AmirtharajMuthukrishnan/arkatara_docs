# Proposed domain data model

Status: TASK 1 FOUNDATIONS IMPLEMENTED; later domain entities and constraints remain proposed for user review. Updated: 2026-09-23.

The implemented foundation covers StaffUser, LegalEntity, BusinessConfiguration and AuditEvent. Their migrations do not implement or approve the later market, catalogue, trial, inventory, pricing or finance designs below. Task 2 remains unstarted.

Approved invariants come from [BUSINESS_RULES.md](BUSINESS_RULES.md). Every open decision remains unresolved in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). Proposed fields, entity boundaries and constraint mechanisms below require design review; they must not be interpreted as choosing deposit, price, stock, hub, payment, return or legal policies.

## Modeling principles and list review

Retain the roadmap's business entities, with `DeliveryAgent` modeled as a staff role and optional profile rather than a second authentication system. No mandatory Customer account entity is needed for guest bookings. The normal booking aggregates boxes and visit fulfillment; a box is not a separate checkout or sale.

Add the following **PROPOSED** concepts because the minimum entity list does not fully explain reservation races, custody, financial allocation and reconciliation. These are logical responsibilities first; implementation may combine small structures where the invariants remain clear.

| Proposed addition | Why it is needed | Decision gates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `InventoryReservation` / unit commitment and `TrialItemAllocation` | Distinguish requested variants from exact committed pieces; record hold timing, release and fulfillment without an ambiguous product-level availability flag. | BUSINESS DECISION BD-02, BD-03, BD-08, BD-09. |
| Custody/handover and QC evidence | Reconstruct who physically held a unit, where it was received and why it was returned to availability. May be typed movement/QC records rather than an elaborate location framework. | BUSINESS DECISION BD-09; LEGAL REVIEW LR-01. |
| `DispatchManifest` / `DispatchDocument` | Describe the combined visit shipment, exact units and reviewed transport documentation independently of a sale invoice. | BUSINESS DECISION BD-07, BD-08; CA REVIEW CA-02; LEGAL REVIEW LR-03. |
| Versioned policy and price calculation snapshots | Preserve the approved rules and calculation inputs used at commitment and purchase without fixing the price-lock point. | BUSINESS DECISION BD-01, BD-02, BD-03, BD-04, BD-12, BD-13. |
| `PaymentContext` and `PaymentAllocation` / deposit application | Bind QR and URL to the same PaymentAttempt and amount/version, support traceable receipts and credit application, and distinguish obligations from provider attempts. | BUSINESS DECISION BD-01, BD-02, BD-05, BD-06, BD-15; CA REVIEW CA-01, CA-02. |
| `GatewayEvent`, `GatewaySettlement`, `BankSettlement` and reconciliation links | Support callback deduplication, fees/net receipts and matching provider evidence to bank receipts. | CA REVIEW CA-02, CA-03. |
| Invoice numbering series and later adjustment-document references | Allocate controlled document identity and preserve corrections without overwriting issued invoices. | CA REVIEW CA-02, CA-03; LEGAL REVIEW LR-03. |
| After-sale case/return authorization | Separate a request, eligibility decision, goods return and refund from ordinary unpurchased trial returns. May remain deferred until policy is approved. | BUSINESS DECISION BD-10; CA REVIEW CA-02; LEGAL REVIEW LR-01. |

Do not create every table immediately. [TASKS.md](TASKS.md) determines sequencing after approval. Use explicit relationships for financial and inventory records; a generic JSON event must not replace necessary referential integrity.

## Reference and operational configuration

| Entity | Responsibility and relationships | Important constraints and history |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `LegalEntity` | Identifies an operating, owning or selling legal party where approved. Referenced by stock ownership evidence, purchases, provider accounts and issued documents as relevant. | Stable internal identifier. Name, addresses and registration details may change prospectively, but historical documents snapshot the applicable issuer. Do not force one permanent company or equate hub location with stock ownership. BUSINESS DECISION BD-11; CA REVIEW CA-03; LEGAL REVIEW LR-02. |
| `BusinessConfiguration` (Task 1 foundation) | Typed, versioned rule proposal with exact scope, effective dates and explicit approval evidence. No configured production values or activation UI are supplied. | Normal ORM/Admin operations append revisions rather than overwrite them. Scope identity is derived from the exact dimensions; no fallback precedence. Approved lookup rejects missing policy and conflicting eligible revisions. Existing legacy rows receive no invented approval. Live activation/retirement remains gated until a reviewed workflow exists. |
| `Market` | City/operating-market identity, code and activation status. Has hubs, service areas and applicable offering configuration. | Unique stable code. Bengaluru ACTIVE and Hyderabad COMING_SOON are initial data intent, not schema enumerations or hard-coded city checks. |
| `Hub` | Operational inventory location associated with a market. | Unique hub code within an explicitly chosen code namespace; market association required. Preserve movement history if a unit changes hub. Do not assume one hub per city or one permanent hub per unit. |
| `ServiceArea` | Data-driven serviceability for a market, such as postal/PIN identifiers or later geographic representation, with active/effective configuration. | Validate identifier format and prevent contradictory active rules in a configured scope. Hub routing and overlapping-service-area precedence need BUSINESS DECISION BD-07/BD-08 before constraints force an answer. |
| `Material` | Material identity and activation, initially Silver active and Gold coming soon. | Unique stable code; not restricted to two values by a database enumeration. |
| `Purity` | Purity standard separate from material, such as S925 or an approved Gold purity. | Proposed material relation and uniqueness of `(material, code)`; validate material/purity compatibility. Examples 14K/18K/22K do not approve the launch assortment (BD-12). |
| `Category` | Product category identity, activation and configurable trial eligibility. | Unique stable code. Eligibility may vary by approved market/material policy; do not scatter category logic in UI conditionals. |
| `TrialPlan` | Versioned/effective offering rules scoped to applicable market, category and optional material dimensions. Describes approved counting/limits and deposit-policy reference. | Plan revision identity immutable once relied upon; new effective revisions for changes. Category scope is explicit. A material scope must not silently prohibit mixed-material boxes (BD-03). Deposit basis and values remain BD-01; counting and initial limits remain BD-03/BD-13. |

Business configuration needs explicit incomplete/unapproved status. The absence of a deposit policy does not mean zero deposit, and missing limits do not mean unlimited boxes.

The implemented Task 1 setting keys are `trials.maximum-boxes` (positive integer), `cart.expiry-seconds` (positive integer), `inventory.reservation-timeout-seconds` (positive integer), and `trials.category-eligibility` (boolean). These technical types and units supply no approved values. Corresponding decisions remain BD-13 and BD-02; deposit/tax/pricing policies are not part of this registry yet. A registered key is not permission to activate it.

## Catalogue and pricing

| Entity | Responsibility and relationships | Important constraints and history |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Product` | Catalogue design, category, description and merchandising identity. Has selectable variants and media. | Stable design code unique within its chosen namespace. One category governs category-specific trial validation. Category changes must not rewrite historical line descriptions or box contents. |
| `ProductVariant` | Selectable configuration of a design: size and applicable material/purity/specification. Not a unique physical jewellery piece. | Unique SKU; business behavior must not parse the SKU. Proposed uniqueness of a normalized option combination within a product once the variant options are defined. Product and variant must agree on category; purity must be compatible with material. |
| `ProductMedia` | Main/additional image, video and thumbnail references, ordering and accessibility/metadata. | Object-storage key/reference independent of a particular host URL. Validate ownership and media purpose. Do not assume one media type or place large binaries in catalogue rows. |
| Pricing configuration / calculation | Supports FIXED and WEIGHT_BASED modes with separately typed/versioned input components such as quantity, weight/unit, rate, fees, currency and tax references when applicable. | No approved Gold formula, metal-rate source, rounding method or lock time is implied. Server-calculated transaction snapshots must contain enough inputs and outcomes to explain the amount. BUSINESS DECISION BD-04/BD-12; CA REVIEW CA-02. |

Use exact decimal values and explicit currency/units for monetary and weight values. Integer minor-unit amounts may be used at provider boundaries with explicit conversion. Numeric precision and rounding need review before implementation, particularly for WEIGHT_BASED calculations; never use binary floating-point for persisted money.

## Physical inventory and custody

| Entity | Responsibility and relationships | Important constraints and indexes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `InventoryUnit` | Internal identity for one physical piece, linked to a variant, current operational hub/market, lifecycle/condition and relevant ownership evidence. | Unique internal ID always; a business serial/barcode scheme remains BD-09. Prevent incompatible active commitments. Index `(hub, variant, status)` and operational availability queries; expose market availability through the hub relationship. SOLD is not available stock. |
| `InventoryMovement` | Append-only change/custody evidence: unit, action, from/to state/location/custodian, event time, record time, actor, reason and causal booking/assignment/document reference. | Ordered per-unit history; unique idempotency/causal key for retried business action where applicable. Index `(unit, occurred_at)` and causal reference. Corrections append evidence; they do not delete old movements. |
| `InventoryReservation` | Proposed committed or time-limited allocation of exact unit(s), related booking context, expiry, policy version and release/consumption evidence. | Database-enforced at most one incompatible active unit commitment, backed by locked workflow validation. Index live expiry and booking context. Reservation acquisition and timeout rules remain BD-02. |
| `TrialItemAllocation` | Proposed linkage between requested `TrialBoxItem` and supplied unit/reservation, including replacement/release history. | Unit and requested variant/category must match the approved fulfillment rule. Do not overwrite an original allocation to conceal a substitution; customer agreement rules remain BD-03/BD-09. |
| QC / custody evidence | Proposed receipt, inspection result, condition, actor, time, evidence references and reviewed disposition. | Actual hub receipt precedes return QC release. A passing result and authorized release are required before availability. Failed QC cannot imply disposal, liability charge or automatic repair policy (BD-09, LR-01). |

Hub is the operational home of stock; current physical custody may be with an agent, in transit, at a visit or at a hub. Do not overload one `hub_id` or one `status` to claim both custody and ownership. A unit can move hubs only through recorded workflows; intercity fulfillment is not an approved fallback. Within-city multi-hub booking fulfillment remains BUSINESS DECISION BD-08.

An original sale remains historical even if an approved after-sale return later makes the same physical unit saleable again. Therefore, do not impose global lifetime uniqueness of an InventoryUnit across all PurchaseItems. Enforce incompatible *active* commitments and preserve successive sale/return events instead; enabling resale still depends on BD-09/BD-10.

## Trial, visit and staff entities

| Entity | Responsibility and relationships | Important constraints and indexes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `TrialBooking` | Guest customer's booking intent/commitment, selected market, contact/address snapshot, policy references, visit progress and correlation/reference identity. Contains multiple boxes. | One normal combined home visit. No mandatory customer account. Unique public reference separate from internal identity; no uniqueness on mobile number. Index `(market, state, created_at)` and approved operational schedule queries. Address/contact access must be restricted. |
| `TrialBox` | One category-specific container within a booking, with applicable plan/policy snapshot and stable box identity. | Exactly one parent booking and category. **Do not add unique `(booking, category)`**: repeated category boxes remain BD-03. Category does not automatically imply exactly one material. |
| `TrialBoxItem` | Requested variant/selection and quantity/counting facts within one box, linked to later exact-unit allocations. | Every item must satisfy its box category and approved plan. Counting designs/sizes/pieces and duplicate quantities remain BD-03; do not fix one-unit-per-row versus quantity semantics prematurely. |
| `StaffUser` / `DeliveryAgent` | Authenticated staff, roles and permissions; optional one-to-one operational agent profile if extra agent details are needed. | Reuse one identity system. Assignment and market/hub scope are enforced by authorization, not just a role label. Deactivate accounts without deleting history. |
| `DeliveryAssignment` | Agent assignment and visit/dispatch progress for a booking, with assignment, reassignment and outcome evidence. | Do not fix lifetime unique `booking_id`: reassignment/history and possible later approved revisit need representation. Normally one active combined visit; concurrent or repeat visit behavior remains BD-07. Index `(agent, status, scheduled_at)` where scheduling is approved. |
| `ArrivalOTP` | Hashed arrival-verification challenge related to the active visit/assignment, generation/expiry, attempts and one-time verification evidence. | No plaintext retained in routine records/logs. Atomic consume/attempt control. One challenge can be delivered on two channels; no separate purchase OTP. Resend/replacement handling remains BD-15 and must not invent revisit policy (BD-07). |
| `DispatchManifest` / `DispatchDocument` | Proposed exact-unit manifest spanning the booking's boxes, linked to dispatch custody and relevant reviewed document snapshot. | Manifest composition must match allocations and selected city. Do not require one originating hub forever or authorize multi-hub sourcing; BD-08 selects the rule. Production format, identity and timing: CA-02/LR-03. |

Cross-row rules such as category agreement, allocation eligibility, market/hub consistency and permitted active assignments require explicit transactional validation and appropriate database enforcement during implementation. A foreign key alone does not enforce those rules.

## Purchase, money and financial records

| Entity | Responsibility and relationships | Important constraints and history |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Purchase` | Backend-calculated selected sale proposal and, after confirmation, historical sale facts: customer, responsible entity, source booking/visit, version, currency, totals and pricing/tax snapshots. | Booking can exist without a purchase. Do not silently add lifetime one-purchase-per-booking or one-purchase-per-box uniqueness; exact outcome/revision cardinality remains BD-05. Shared boxes do not imply separate checkouts. |
| `PurchaseItem` | Selected physical piece/quantity with description, SKU, material/purity, quantity/weight, pricing components, discounts if approved, tax basis/rates/amounts and line totals at sale. | References catalogue and exact unit evidence but does not derive historical values from current catalogue. No duplicate active sale allocation for one piece. Confirmed lines are preserved; later correction is separately recorded. |
| `BookingDeposit` | Separate deposit obligation/policy context and collection/application/refund facts associated with a booking and optional approved basis. | Do not force one deposit per booking or per box; do not equate deposit with a discount. Represent collected, applied, refunded and unresolved balance distinctly. Commercial scope: BD-01. Financial treatment: CA-01. |
| `PaymentContext` | Proposed payable obligation/version and canonical QR/link access context, related to a deposit or purchase purpose with currency, amount due and expiry/version evidence. | Exactly identified obligation; avoid ambiguous nullable references or unconstrained generic references. Both payment surfaces resolve the same current final PaymentAttempt. A shared context is not permission for two independent collectible attempts. No box-driven independent payment contexts. |
| `PaymentAttempt` | One collection attempt under a payment context; server-computed amount/currency, provider/account, status and verification evidence. | Unique scoped idempotency key. Context can retain attempt history; number/retry/replacement behavior remains BD-05. Reject stale amount/version requests under the approved rule rather than changing an issued amount silently. |
| `GatewayTransaction` | Verified external authorization/capture/payment/refund evidence associated with provider account and attempt where identifiable. | Unique `(provider, account, transaction_reference, transaction_kind)` or provider-equivalent namespace, finalized after adapter contract review. Retain unmatched/late receipts for reconciliation. Provider success and business acceptance are distinct. |
| `PaymentAllocation` / deposit application | Proposed append-only linkage of verified money/credit to a financial obligation, with amount, currency, time and reversals/corrections where approved. | Lock receipt/obligation totals to prevent over-allocation. A deposit application must not change jewellery consideration. Exact recognition/ledger treatment remains CA-01/CA-02. |
| `Refund` | Independent repayment request, approval/evidence, source transaction/allocation, reason, provider references and verified outcome. | Idempotency key; no over-refund after accounting for succeeded and in-flight refunds. Partial-refund policy and eligibility remain BD-01/BD-10 and CA-01/CA-02/LR-01. A refund does not move inventory. |
| `GatewayEvent` | Proposed verified webhook/callback identity, received time, digest/necessary payload evidence and processing result. | Unique provider-account event reference where available; fallback deduplication contract reviewed per provider (BD-15). Restrict sensitive payloads and retention (BD-15, LR-04). Callback event storage alone does not prove payment without verification. |
| `GatewaySettlement` | Proposed provider settlement reference, covered transactions, fees/adjustments and net settlement evidence. | Unique scoped provider settlement reference. Many transactions can reconcile to a settlement, with explicit allocations where needed. Do not assume one-to-one payment-to-settlement. |
| `BankSettlement` / reconciliation match | Proposed imported bank receipt/reference evidence and matching links to provider settlements or other approved collections. | Preserve unmatched, partial and aggregated matches; uniqueness must use a reliable source/import identity, not amount/date alone. CA-02/CA-03 review accounting presentation. |
| `TaxCode` | Versioned classification/HSN and applicable tax calculation configuration with effective scope/dates. | No hard-coded GST rate. Detect conflicting effective configurations within the approved scope. Applicability, component rates and rounding are CA-02. |
| `TaxInvoice` | Issued, immutable sale document including issuer/customer details, controlled number, issue date, tax/place-of-supply facts as reviewed, currency, totals and linked purchase evidence. | Unique issued number within the CA-approved series/entity/period scope; allocate atomically. No global one-invoice-per-booking rule. Historical issuer and totals cannot be recomputed from current entity/catalogue. CA-02/CA-03/LR-03 determine exact requirements. |
| `TaxInvoiceItem` | Immutable invoice line descriptions, quantities/weights, classification, prices, tax breakdown and totals. | Belongs to one invoice; stable line identity/order. Snapshot data agrees with the issued document, independently of later edits to catalogue or configuration. |
| Invoice series / adjustment document | Proposed controlled numbering scope and separately linked credit/debit/correction evidence where reviewed. | Preserve issued number/history, including void/correction evidence as legally/accountingly required. Do not overwrite invoices or invent a legal correction method (CA-02, LR-03). |

Historic financial records include confirmed Purchase/PurchaseItem snapshots, verified GatewayTransactions, monetary allocations, deposit applications, issued TaxInvoice/TaxInvoiceItems, executed refunds, settlement evidence and later financial adjustments. Working proposals and attempts may progress, but their transition/evidence history remains attributable. An invoice can show INR 5,000 jewellery total, INR 49 deposit applied and INR 4,951 remaining without redefining jewellery price as INR 4,951; these are illustrative amounts, not approved policy.

## Audit, notification and analytical evidence

| Entity | Responsibility | Constraints and indexes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AuditEvent` | Append-only attributable privileged action: actor/system identity, action, subject, time, correlation, reason and relevant change evidence. | Index subject/time and actor/time. Restrict write/update/delete paths. Avoid plaintext OTPs, secrets or unnecessary full personal-data copies. A financial record is not replaced by an audit log. |
| `NotificationEvent` | Business notification intent and provider delivery/status evidence; references booking/payment/invoice/arrival challenge as needed. | Unique notification idempotency scope for intended sends; separate attempts/status history. ## Relationships and constraints that must remain explicit

- Market has hubs and service areas; a physical unit has an operational hub and a product variant. Availability is scoped to the selected market's approved fulfillment operation.
- Product has variants and media. Material and Purity are distinct. Category is the single-category boundary of a TrialBox.
- TrialBooking has TrialBoxes; each has TrialBoxItems; allocation maps requested contents to exact pieces. One normal booking supplies one customer home visit with all its category boxes fulfilled together.
- Assignment history relates staff to that combined visit. No lifetime uniqueness that erases reassignment, and no automatic support for concurrent split visits.
- Purchases, deposit contexts, payment attempts, allocations, invoices, refunds and settlements remain separate. Their links explain the money and goods history without forcing a commercial cardinality that has not been approved.
- Use protected/restricted deletion for referenced historical records. Retire configuration and staff prospectively. Any personal-data retention/anonymization design must preserve required financial evidence and receive LEGAL REVIEW LR-04, coordinated with CA-02/CA-03, before production; it must not delete history accidentally through cascading relations. Operational retention settings remain BUSINESS DECISION BD-15.
- Timestamps should distinguish occurrence, recording, expiry, confirmation and issuance where relevant. Use timezone-aware storage and explicit display time zones; do not encode business deadlines from the server's local clock.
- Candidate indexes also include reservation expiry/status, active unit commitments, booking reference, payment context/status, gateway references, unprocessed events, outstanding refunds, invoice issuer/series/date and reconciliation status. Final indexes follow actual queries; do not add every field as an index.
- Decide integrity enforcement consciously: ordinary unique/check constraints for row-local facts, conditional uniqueness for applicable active records, locked services for cross-row totals, and migrations/tests to preserve those guarantees. These are proposed mechanisms, not generated DDL.

Before implementing a policy-dependent constraint, check [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). In particular, do not add unique booking/category boxes, lifetime single-hub routing, automatic deposit defaults, a fixed price-lock point, global one-sale-ever-per-unit, or exact booking/purchase/deposit cardinalities merely for implementation convenience.
